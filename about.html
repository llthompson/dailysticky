Daily Sticky - The Digital Sticker Journal

A Journal for People Who Don’t Want to Journal

What is Daily Sticky?

Daily Sticky is a free, calendar-based daily journal that captures your life with stickers and short notes. It’s a visual diary that lets you see your month and year at a glance, so you can remember the little moments, moods, memories, and patterns that made up your days.

Daily Sticky is simple on purpose.

With Daily Sticky, you don’t have to write long entries, track every detail of your life, or turn remembering your days into yet another chore you fall behind on. Instead of trying to document your entire life, you pick one sticker that captures how the day felt and add a few words.

It's a journal where stickers tell your story.

How Daily Sticky works

Each day, you open your calendar and choose one sticker for that date.

Then, if you want, you can write a few sentences to share the story behind the sticker.

Over time, your calendar becomes a visual record of your year.

Why stickers?

Because they’re cute. Because we all need a little whimsy in our lives. Because a picture can say a thousand words that you don’t have time to write.

With stickers, there's no obligation to be profound or even totally accurate. It only has to mean something to you. Maybe it represents your mood or your dreams or your lunch. Maybe you just thought it was cute. You're in charge, and that's the point.

Why it’s different from regular journaling

Traditional journaling can feel like a lot: blank pages, long entries, pressure to catch up when you miss a day, pressure to explain everything.

And digital journals, while more convenient, still try to do everything. They become planners, scrapbooks, and productivity systems all at once.

Daily Sticky is smaller than that on purpose.

You are not documenting your entire life. You are not trying to accomplish everything. You are choosing one little image for the day and giving yourself enough context to remember why it mattered to you.

What you can look back on

Daily Sticky lets you see your week, your month, and your year at a glance. Your memories aren't hidden inside pages of text you may never reread. You see them all, cute and funny and chaotic, every time you open your calendar.

When you can see your whole year of stickers at once, it's easy to look back and reflect on your journey. You can notice patterns, remember moments you might have forgotten, and get a visual snapshot of what your life actually felt like, one day at a time.
