About Daily Sticky

The Daily Sticker Diary is a simple, visual way to keep a daily diary using cute stickers. Instead of writing long entries, you log each day with a single sticker and an optional note.

It’s designed to make journaling feel easy, consistent, and actually enjoyable— something you can stick to without overthinking it.

How it works

  • Tap a day
  • Choose a sticker
  • Add a short note (optional)

Features

  • Daily sticker journaling
  • Optional short notes
  • Weekly recap sharing
  • Private, local data storage
  • Export and import your data anytime
  • No logins, no accounts needed

Why Daily Sticky

Most journaling apps ask for too much. Daily Sticky keeps it simple— one small moment per day, captured visually. It's cute, fun, and fast.

Whether you're tracking your mood, your goals, or just your life, this is a lightweight way to build a daily habit.
