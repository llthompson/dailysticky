About Daily Sticky

One day, one sticker. Journaling on easy mode.

If you love the idea of journaling but never stick with it, this is for you. Daily Sticky is a digital sticker diary that lets you remember your year without writing paragraphs or feeling behind.

It’s like a “year in pixels,” but with stickers. Tap a day, pick a vibe, done.

At the end of the week, you can turn your stickers into a shareable recap— a cute little snapshot of your days you can keep or send to friends.

No pressure. No overthinking. Just a habit you might actually keep—and a year you can actually see.

How it works

  • Tap a day
  • Choose a sticker
  • Add a short note (optional)

Features

  • Daily sticker journaling
  • Optional short notes
  • Weekly recap sharing
  • Private, local data storage
  • Export and import your data anytime
  • No logins, no accounts needed

Why Daily Sticky

Most journaling apps ask for too much. Daily Sticky keeps it simple— one small moment per day, captured visually.

Whether you're tracking your mood, your habits, or just your life, this is a lightweight way to build a journaling habit you can actually stick to.
