Your Tiny Daily Ritual

A Daily Ritual for the Wordle Obsessed

If you love tiny daily games, Daily Sticky is another fun little habit to add to your day, except this one is all about you.

For people who love games like Wordle

If you are always looking for games like Wordle, the best daily word games, daily puzzles, streaks, check-ins, or little rituals that make the day feel marked in some way, Daily Sticky fits right into that same rhythm.

It's not another word game, but it does give you that same satisfying daily moment: open it, make one tiny choice, and feel like you did your little thing for the day.

It only takes a few minutes, but it gives the day a tiny moment of structure. One little puzzle. One little ritual. One little “okay, I did my thing today.”

That's the kind of habit Daily Sticky was made for.

Daily Sticky isn't a game, but it scratches the same itch

Daily Sticky is a digital sticker journal where you pick one sticker for each day.

That's it. That's the ritual.

You open the app, tap the day, choose the sticker that matches the vibe, and save it. Maybe you add a note if the day deserves a little lore. Maybe you don't. Either way, future you gets a tiny record of what happened.

Except this daily habit's for you

Daily games are fun because they give you a tiny challenge to come back to. Daily Sticky gives you a tiny check-in to come back to.

Not for points. Not for a leaderboard. Not because an app is trying to make you optimize your life into a spreadsheet.

It's for your moods, your memories, your tiny wins, your cozy nights, your errands, your spirals, your emotional side quests, and your “what even was that?” days.

One sticker a day is enough

The best part is that it doesn't need to become a whole thing.

You don't have to write a long journal entry. You don't have to explain your entire day. You don't have to be profound about the fact that you got coffee, answered emails, saw a weird dog, and somehow survived Tuesday.

You can pick one sticker and be done in seconds.

And then your year starts turning into something

One daily sticker doesn't feel like much at first. But after a week, you have a tiny recap. After a month, you have a little pattern. After a few months, you can start seeing the shape of your year.

The busy weeks. The cozy phases. The social stretches. The quiet days. The “why was I like this?” moments. The tiny memories that would have disappeared if you had not saved them somewhere.

It's like a daily game, a visual diary, and a tiny memory jar all rolled into one cute little calendar.

Perfect for people who like a little daily habit

If you already love daily word games, daily puzzles, streaks, check-ins, or little rituals that make the day feel marked in some way, Daily Sticky fits right into that same rhythm.

Open it once a day. Pick the sticker. Add a note if you want. Move on with your life.

And if you miss a day? Truly, nothing dramatic has to happen. Fill it in later if you remember. Skip it if you want. This isn't homework. This is your tiny ritual.
