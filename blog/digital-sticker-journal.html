Digital Sticker Journal

What Is a Digital Sticker Journal?

A digital sticker journal is a cute, low-effort way to remember your days without writing full journal entries.

A new way to journal

Traditional journaling can be meaningful, but it can also feel like a lot to keep up with. Daily Sticky takes a different approach: it turns journaling into one delightful daily choice, using a sticker to capture the mood, memory, or little moment you want to remember.

This is not about decorating a journal after you write in it. The sticker is the way you journal. Instead of trying to describe everything that happened, you choose one sticker to represent your day. Over time, those stickers turn into a visual record of your year.

How Daily Sticky works

Daily Sticky is an easy mobile site you can use right from your phone. There are no logins, and your sticker journal is saved to your local device storage, so it stays simple, private, and low-pressure.

Using it is three simple steps:

  1. Pick one sticker that matches your day.
  2. Add an optional tiny note if you want to remember more.
  3. Save it to your calendar and watch your year fill up over time.

It's designed for people who like the idea of journaling, mood tracking, memory keeping, or a year in pixels, but want something cuter and easier to use every day.

Who it's for

Daily Sticky is for people who want to remember their lives without turning journaling into a big project. It’s especially fun for artists, creatives, visual thinkers, neurodivergent journalers, sticker lovers, and anyone who wants a cute, low-pressure way to track their days.
