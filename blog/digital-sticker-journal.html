Digital Sticker Journal

What Is a Digital Sticker Journal?

A digital sticker journal is a cute, low-effort way to remember your days without writing full journal entries.

A simpler way to journal

Traditional journaling can be meaningful, but it can also feel like a lot to keep up with. A digital sticker journal makes the habit easier by letting you choose one sticker to represent your day.

Instead of writing a long entry, you can pick a sticker that matches your mood, memory, activity, or tiny moment. Over time, those stickers turn into a visual record of your year.

How Daily Sticky works

Daily Sticky is a digital sticker journal where you pick one sticker a day and place it on your calendar. You can also add an optional note if you want to remember more detail.

It's designed for people who like the idea of journaling, mood tracking, memory keeping, or a year in pixels, but want something cuter and easier to use every day.

Who it's for

A digital sticker journal is great for anyone who wants to remember their year without turning journaling into a big project. It can be used as a visual diary, a daily journal app, a memory keeping tool, or a cute year in pixels alternative.

Start your own sticker journal

With Daily Sticky, you can start small. Pick one sticker, save the day, and let your year build one tiny memory at a time.
