Year in Pixels Alternative

Year in Pixels vs. Sticker Journal

A year in pixels is simple and cute. A sticker journal gives your days a little more personality.

What is a year in pixels?

A year in pixels is a simple journaling idea where each day of the year gets one colored square. Most people use the colors to track moods, habits, energy, or the overall feeling of the day.

It's popular because it's easy to keep up with. You don't have to write a full journal entry. You choose a color, fill in the day, and slowly watch your year turn into a visual pattern.

So what is a sticker journal?

A sticker journal works in a similar way, but instead of choosing a color, you choose a sticker. That tiny difference makes the whole habit feel more personal, more playful, and a little more fun to look back on.

A color can tell you that a day was happy, stressful, calm, or weird. A sticker can remind you what made the day feel that way.

Maybe it was a coffee date. Maybe it was an overthinking spiral. Maybe it was a tiny win, a cozy night, a sad panda kind of day, or one of those “I survived, somehow” moments.

Year in pixels tracks the mood. Stickers tell the tiny lore.

A year in pixels is great if you want a simple mood tracker. But sometimes a color feels too vague. When you look back months later, you might remember that a day was “blue” or “yellow,” but not what actually happened.

A sticker journal gives future you a little more context. It still stays quick and low-pressure, but it lets each day have more personality.

Why stickers can be easier to stick with

The best journaling habit's the one you actually want to come back to. Stickers make daily tracking feel less like homework and more like a tiny creative ritual.

You don't have to write a paragraph. You don't have to explain your whole day. You can choose one sticker, add a short note if you want, and move on with your life.

Daily Sticky as a year in pixels alternative

Daily Sticky gives you the simple daily structure of a year in pixels, but with cute digital stickers instead of color squares.

You pick one sticker a day, place it on your calendar, and optionally add a short note. Over time, your calendar becomes a visual diary of your moods, memories, tiny moments, and daily chaos.

It's still easy. It's still quick. It just has a little more charm.

Which one should you use?

Use a year in pixels if you want a very simple color-based mood tracker.

Use a sticker journal if you want something that feels more personal, expressive, and fun to revisit.
